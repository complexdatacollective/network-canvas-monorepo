The Family Pedigree and Narrative Pedigree articles now say that the Symbols choice and changes to wording shared by every stage of a kind are saved with the stage and discarded if you cancel the stage, instead of being saved straight away. The Narrative Pedigree article also explains that the attribute list for a disease shows the person type's other attributes under **Not available here**, and that the nomination prompt for a disease is added to the source stage.
