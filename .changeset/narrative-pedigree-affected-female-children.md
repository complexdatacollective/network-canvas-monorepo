In the Narrative Pedigree, for an X-linked recessive condition, the children of a woman recorded as affected now always get a status: her daughters may carry the condition and her sons may develop it, and the same continues down through her daughters. Before, her children were only marked when her own mother had been worked out to be a carrier, so whether they were marked depended on which other relatives had been recorded. Nothing new is worked out about her parents.
