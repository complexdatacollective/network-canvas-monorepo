The Deploying Fresco guide has a step-by-step walkthrough to follow while you deploy. Start it from the card at the top of the guide, or open it at any section with **Walk through this step**. It goes at your pace: each step waits until you confirm it, asks about your setup (a GitHub account, passkey or password, UploadThing or your own S3 bucket, and whether you have a protocol to upload), shows only the steps that apply, and remembers where you left off. Every step shows the screen you'll see, a recording of Fresco's setup, or an illustration, and with reduced motion they appear without animation.

The guide also now says to enter your password twice, to follow your device's prompt when you choose a passkey, and to wait for **Protocol imported successfully** before clicking **Continue**.
