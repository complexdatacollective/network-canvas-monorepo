The Fresco dashboard is now available in Arabic (العربية), its first
right-to-left language. Choose it from the language menu, or let Fresco follow
a browser that prefers Arabic; every dashboard screen is then laid out from
right to left. This is the language of the dashboard itself: the languages
participants take an interview in are set by the protocol.

The Arabic is Modern Standard Arabic. It was checked by reverse translation
and has not yet been reviewed by a native speaker, so corrections are welcome.
