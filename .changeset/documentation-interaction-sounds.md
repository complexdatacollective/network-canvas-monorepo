The Using Fresco and Using Interviewer pages describe the new setting that plays sounds during interviews: which actions play a sound, that the setting is off by default and how to turn it on, that sounds play at the volume set on the participant's device, and that no sound plays until the participant first taps, clicks or presses a key. The Interviewer pages also explain that the sounds work offline and that each device has its own setting. The About Fresco page no longer lists interaction sounds as a missing feature. All of these pages are updated in English, Spanish and French.
