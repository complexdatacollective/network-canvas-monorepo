Interviewer is now available in Arabic (العربية), its first right-to-left
language. Choose it from the language setting, or let Interviewer follow a
browser that prefers Arabic; every screen is then laid out from right to left.
As with the other languages, a protocol's own text stays in the language it
was written in.

The Arabic is Modern Standard Arabic. It was checked by reverse translation
and has not yet been reviewed by a native speaker, so corrections are welcome.
