In the Family Pedigree, adding a sibling as an adopted child of the parents they share no longer asks whether they are a twin or who carried the pregnancy. Someone else carried them, so the parents who adopted them are recorded as not having carried them, and they are not anyone's twin. The twin question still appears when the participant was adopted by the same parents, since twins can be adopted together. If a parent who adopted someone is later changed to another kind of parent, the recorded answer that they did not carry the pregnancy is cleared and the question is asked again.
