The Fresco deployment guide has an animated walkthrough of every step, from
forking Fresco to uploading a first protocol. A card at the top of the guide
opens the whole walkthrough in a full-screen dialog, and each section has a
"Watch this step" button that opens it at that step. From inside the
walkthrough, "Read this step in the guide" closes it and moves to the matching
section of the guide.
