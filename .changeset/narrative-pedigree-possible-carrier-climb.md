When a Narrative Pedigree stage shows possible statuses for an X-linked recessive condition, the possible-carrier status now climbs the mother's line for as many generations as the pedigree records. Above the mother of an affected male, and above every woman shown as carrying the condition, her mother and the sisters who share her mother may carry it and the brothers who share her mother may develop it, and the same applies again from her mother. Before, only the mother, maternal grandmother, maternal aunts and maternal uncles of an affected male were marked, so a woman three or more generations above the affected males could have no status even when two of her daughters were shown as carrying the condition.

The climb stops above a woman who is not affected and whose recorded father is affected, because his X accounts for hers. Her mother was marked before when the woman had an affected son, and is no longer marked.
