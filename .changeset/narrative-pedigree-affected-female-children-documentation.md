The Narrative Pedigree page, in English, Spanish and French, now explains three things about X-linked recessive conditions: the daughters of an affected woman may carry the condition and her sons may develop it, and her being affected adds nothing about her parents; a woman with an affected son and an affected mother carries it; and the path of a woman who carries it and whose father is affected runs only through her father, unless she is herself affected.
