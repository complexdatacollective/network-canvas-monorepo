The Narrative Pedigree page, in English, Spanish and French, now explains three things about X-linked recessive conditions: the daughters of an affected woman may carry the condition and her sons may develop it, and on its own her being affected adds nothing about her parents; a woman carries the condition when it appears on two sides of her, a side being an affected son, the lines of her daughters by one father, or her mother's family, with the conditions on the fathers of the females in between; and the path of a woman who carries it and whose father is affected runs only through her father, unless she is herself affected.
