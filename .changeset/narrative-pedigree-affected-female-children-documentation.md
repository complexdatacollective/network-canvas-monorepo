The Narrative Pedigree page, in English, Spanish and French, now explains three things about X-linked recessive conditions: the daughters of an affected woman may carry the condition and her sons may develop it, and on its own her being affected adds nothing about her parents; a woman carries the condition when it appears on two sides of her, in the lines of two of her children or in one child's line and in her mother's family, and the women between her and those relatives carry it too; and the path of a woman who carries it and whose father is affected runs only through her father, unless she is herself affected.
