Stages no longer have an introduction panel. In the protocol editor, Per
Alter Form, Per Alter Edge Form, Dyad Census and Tie-Strength Census stages
no longer offer an introduction section; instructions for participants go in
an Information stage instead. An Ego Form's heading is now the title of its
form, which the form section asks for.

A stored schema 8 protocol is upgraded with each introduction panel turned
into an Information stage just before its stage, with the panel's title and
text and the stage's skip logic. An Ego Form's panel title becomes its form
title, and its text, if any, becomes an Information stage before it.
