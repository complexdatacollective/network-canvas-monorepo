Architect is now available in Arabic (العربية), its first right-to-left
language. Choose it from the language menu, or let Architect follow a browser
that prefers Arabic; every screen is then laid out from right to left. This is
the language of Architect itself: the languages a protocol is written in are
separate, and are still set on the protocol's Languages page.

The Arabic is Modern Standard Arabic. It was checked by reverse translation
and has not yet been reviewed by a native speaker, so corrections are welcome.
