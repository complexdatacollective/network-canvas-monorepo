Option labels now show their formatting everywhere Architect lists them: the choice values of a categorical or ordinal attribute, the read-only option tables, and the shape chosen for each value. Bold and italic text appear as formatted, and characters such as `<`, `&` and `'` appear as typed instead of as `**`, `&lt;`, `&amp;` or `&#39;`.
