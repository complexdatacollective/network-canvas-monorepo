Translating Your Protocol, Protocol Schema Information, the Fresco and
Interviewer guides and the stage pages now explain that a protocol
holds only the wording you change. Network Canvas's own wording for stage
settings and interview text is shown by the interview, and by Architect's
stage editors and translation table, without being written into your
protocol. Adding a language therefore writes nothing for that wording, and a
correction to Network Canvas's wording reaches every protocol that has not
changed that text. Clearing a cell of that wording in the translation table
puts Network Canvas's wording back. The closing text of a Finish Screen and the answer labels
of a Family Pedigree are still written into your protocol as a starting
point. The Family Pedigree page says that the name question's guidance is
always shown.
