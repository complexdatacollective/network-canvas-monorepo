The Family Pedigree has Undo and Redo buttons in its toolbar, so a participant can take back their last change to their family and bring it back again. Ctrl+Z (⌘Z on a Mac) undoes and Ctrl+Shift+Z (⇧⌘Z) or Ctrl+Y redoes. Each saved change is one step: adding someone, saving their details, removing someone, connecting or disconnecting two people, answering a checklist question, or selecting someone for a question. The zoom and Show the whole family buttons have moved to a small toolbar of their own beside the main one.
