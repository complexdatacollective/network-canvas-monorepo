The Name Generator (using forms) stage editor has a new **Walk me through it** button. It opens a floating guide that explains what the stage collects and when to use it, then walks through each setting with short animations that show what participants will see. The guide follows you into the windows for node types, form fields, prompts and side panels, and picks up where you left off when you save. You can drag the panel anywhere or minimize it while you work.
