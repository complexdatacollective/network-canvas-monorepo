Stage editors for the name generators, Sociogram, Ordinal Bin, Categorical Bin, Per Alter Form, Ego Form, Per Alter Edge Form, Dyad Census, One to Many Dyad Census, Tie-Strength Census, Narrative, Geospatial, Information and Finish Screen have a new **Walk me through it** button. It opens a floating guide that explains what the stage collects and when to use it, then walks through each setting with short animations that show what participants will see. The guide follows you into the windows a stage opens, such as node types, form fields and prompts, and picks up where you left off when you save. You can drag the panel anywhere, or minimize it to the toolbar while you work.
