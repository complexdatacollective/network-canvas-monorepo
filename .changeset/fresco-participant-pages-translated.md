The pages participants see outside the interview itself now appear in the
participant's language: the page shown when an interview cannot be opened, the
pages for an interview link that fails, no longer works, or needs anonymous
recruitment turned on, the notice shown when the screen is too small, and the
thank-you page at the end. They were always in English before. The thank-you
page follows the language the participant chose inside the interview. The page
that appears when an interview link fails no longer says "onboarding".
