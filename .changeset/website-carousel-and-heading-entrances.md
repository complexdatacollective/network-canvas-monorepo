The homepage is easier to move through.

- **Design Principles** now pages like a carousel: one scroll, swipe or arrow
  key moves exactly one card, however hard you scroll, and the page carries on
  past the first and last cards. A card too tall for the screen scrolls through
  to its end before the next one comes. The illustration plays each card's
  animation when its card arrives, at its own pace, and plays it backwards as
  you go back.
- **Section headings** rise in word by word when they come into view, and then
  stay.
- **Tools**: the Architect, Interviewer and Fresco cards open as the thread
  reaches them, and close again as you scroll back up.
- The label shown when you point at a paper in the publications network has
  more space around its title.

With reduced motion turned on, Design Principles cards still page one at a time but jump
straight to the next card, headings fade in without moving, the
illustration shows each card's finished scene, and links within the page jump
straight to where they lead instead of gliding there.
