Six of Architect's protocol templates now name the person a question is
about, using values the interview fills in. For example, "How often are you
in contact with this person?" becomes "How often are you in contact with
Sam?". If the name is missing, the question reads as it did before. The
life transitions template also shows the date of the change in its network
composer labels.
