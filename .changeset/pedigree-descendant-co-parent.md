The family pedigree no longer fails to draw when a child's parents include one of their own descendants, such as a daughter who carries her mother's baby or a son who donates to his mother and her partner, or when two sets of parents cross one another's lines of descent. Every child is now drawn below each of its parents, including a single parent's children when that parent is moved down a row to sit beside a partner.
