An interview in a right-to-left language, such as Arabic, now lays out its
stages from the right. The panel a relative is added in on a family tree, and
the panel for a person's details in the Network Composer, open from the left
and are swiped away to the left; the arrow keys and the drag that resize the
composer's panel follow. The button that adds a person, the preset controls on
a Narrative stage and the controls over a map sit on the mirrored side, and
arrows that mean "next" or "continue" point the way reading goes. A family
tree is drawn the same way round in every language, so the buttons that add a
relative stay where they were, and the arrow keys move between them as they
are drawn.
