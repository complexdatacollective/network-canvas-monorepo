The wording of the documentation is corrected on every page other than the Piped Text article, which was corrected earlier. Sentences that omitted words such as "that" now include them. A string that can be translated is now called a "translatable string" or a "localized string" and is no longer called "a text". "Contains", "stores" or "includes" replaces "holds". Sentences that described what "the interview" does now say what is shown during the interview. In Translating Your Protocol, the headings "Words Network Canvas supplies", "Changing the language your text is recorded as" and "Choosing which texts and languages to show" are now "Words supplied by Network Canvas", "Changing the language in which your text is recorded" and "Choosing which translatable strings and languages to show". Links to these sections still work. Updated in English, Spanish and French.
