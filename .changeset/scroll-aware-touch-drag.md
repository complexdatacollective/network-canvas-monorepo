On touch screens, dragging people out of a scrolling area now works in the direction you'd expect. In the Sociogram's bin you can drag a person straight up or diagonally onto the canvas, and swiping sideways across the bin scrolls it. In the roster and name generator panels, swiping up and down scrolls and dragging sideways moves a person. To drag along the direction an area scrolls, hold the person still for a moment first.
