The Information interface article describes the new single editor: a page is one text, with its heading at the top and its images, audio and video placed in it as blocks, each with settings for its description, display size, alignment, and whether it starts automatically and repeats. Protocol Schema Information lists the change under schema 9 and says how upgrading a protocol joins a page's heading and items into one text. Updated in English; the Spanish and French pages follow.
