Every stage editor now has a **Walk me through it** button at the top of its
list of sections. It opens a step-by-step guide to the stage's settings in
place of that list: each step points at the setting in the form and explains
what it changes in the interview, and the Network Composer guide shows
screenshots of a real interview with and without each setting. Steps the stage
cannot be saved without wait until they are filled in, short yes-or-no
questions skip the parts a stage will not use, and the last step lists what is
set so far.
