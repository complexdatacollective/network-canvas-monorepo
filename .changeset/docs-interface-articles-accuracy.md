The Information interface article no longer says a screen holds at most four items that are resized into spaces: a screen has a page heading and any number of items, and images and videos take a display size. The Geospatial interface article now gives `outside-selectable-areas` as the value recorded for an answer outside the selectable areas. The Language Chooser article no longer says its stage editor opens the translation table, and now describes the button that records a language's translations as a different language.
