The per-person form now shows the person joined to their form. On a wide screen the person sits beside the form, and on a tall screen above it, staying in view as a long form scrolls. Moving on, the questions clear, the last person shrinks away, the next slides into place, and their questions appear one after another. The form about each connection between two people shows the pair the same way, joined to the top of the form. Moving to the next connection, the line between them is taken back, anyone not in the same place in the next pair shrinks away, the new people drop in one after another, and the line is drawn between them again. With reduced motion turned on, they simply fade.
