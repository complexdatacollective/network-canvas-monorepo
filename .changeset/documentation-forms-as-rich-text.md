The documentation describes forms written as one piece of formatted text. The Forms page explains how to add a field with the **Field** button, the settings of a field, how to arrange a form, how logic blocks show fields only in some cases and clear the answers to hidden fields, and how forms in different languages can contain different fields. The Piped Text page describes the **Attribute** button, dynamic attributes, logic blocks and their logic options, and says that a missing value shows nothing. Its tables of problems are updated to the messages that Architect reports now. The Node Labeling, Prompts, Ego Form, Family Pedigree, Information, Building a Protocol, Translating Your Protocol and Protocol Schema Information pages are updated to match: the Ego Form's introduction is now a heading and text at the top of its form, and a Name Generator's form has a **Dialog title**. The Translating Your Protocol page describes editing the text of a form, logic blocks and dynamic attributes in the translation table, the names of the rows for the versions of a dynamic attribute, and copying the content of a cell, with its fields, from one language to another. The Name Generator, Quick Add, Roster, Categorical Bin, Ordinal Bin, Family Pedigree and Narrative Pedigree pages say that values in built-in wording are inserted with the **Attribute** button. Every change is in English, Spanish and French. The screenshots of forms and of the old toolbar buttons are not updated yet.
