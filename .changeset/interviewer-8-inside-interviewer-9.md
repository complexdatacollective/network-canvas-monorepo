Interviewer 8 can now run inside Interviewer 9, at `/v8/` on the same site, so the protocols and interviews you already have keep working after Interviewer 9 ships. There it runs the interview alone, on the same data: Interviewer 9 lists your schema 8 protocols and interviews, and when you start, resume or review one it runs in Interviewer 8, exactly as before. Leaving or finishing the interview takes you back to Interviewer 9. It uses the same lock as Interviewer 9, so you unlock once, in Interviewer 9 (set the lock up or change it there too).

Importing a protocol made with a newer version of Network Canvas now says so, instead of showing a generic error.
