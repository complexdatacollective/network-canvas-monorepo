Interviewer 8 can now run inside Interviewer 9, at `/v8/` on the same site, so the protocols and interviews you already have keep working after Interviewer 9 ships. Inside Interviewer 9, Interviewer 8 shows a banner that says it is for schema 8 protocols, with a way back to Interviewer. It uses the same lock as Interviewer 9 (set it up or change it in Interviewer 9), and opens the interviews and protocol files Interviewer 9 passes to it.

Importing a protocol made with a newer version of Network Canvas now says so, instead of showing a generic error.
