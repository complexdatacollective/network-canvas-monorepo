Family Pedigree labels that describe someone through a relative no longer capitalise the kinship word after the relative's name: "Ana’s parent" rather than "Ana’s Parent". Languages that put the kinship word first ("Parent de Ana") or capitalise every noun, such as German, are unchanged.
