The Colored Eco-Genetic Relationship Map template marks the people named in "People in your life" as non-kin, and its later stages use that mark to tell them apart from the family on the pedigree. Before, it treated anyone without a gender identity as non-kin, which sorted relatives the participant never described, such as parents the pedigree adds automatically, with the non-kin and broke when a protocol turned gender identity off.
