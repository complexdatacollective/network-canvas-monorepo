Each article now ends with "Did this article help?". Readers can answer Yes or
No and, if they want, say how the article could be improved. Answers go to the
team through the site's analytics, so we can see which articles need work.
Readers can change or take back their Yes or No until they send a comment, and
the site remembers their answer when they come back to the article.
