Each article now ends with "Did you get what you needed from this article?".
Readers can answer Yes or No and, if they want, add a comment. The Yes or No is
recorded as soon as it is pressed, whether or not anything is written. Answers
go to the team through the site's analytics, so we can see which articles need
work. Once a reader sends a comment, the prompt thanks them.
