The Family Pedigree interface page now describes the interface as it works today: how participants add and describe relatives, the person and relationship attributes the stage records, the wording, completeness and nomination prompt settings, and where a person's symbol comes from. The Implementing CEGRM tutorial no longer describes a Narrative Pedigree stage or an attribute that nothing writes, and matches the bundled template's 13 stages. It also explains that the template marks non-kin people with a `non_kin` attribute, instead of using gender identity to tell family from non-kin.
