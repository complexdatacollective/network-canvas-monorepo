The documentation covers the Finish Screen, the stage that ends every
interview. A new interface page explains what participants see before and
after they finish, how to write its heading and text, and how to choose the
outcome it records. The data export and data dictionary pages describe the new
`networkCanvasFinishOutcome` column and `nc:finishOutcome` attribute. The
skip logic, translation, preview, schema 9, Fresco and Interviewer pages are
updated to match, including the Fresco API's new `finishStageId` and
`finishOutcome` fields and the removal of Fresco's separate "interview
finished" page.
