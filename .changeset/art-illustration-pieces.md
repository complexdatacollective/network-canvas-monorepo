The pieces the networkcanvas.com updates illustrations are drawn from are now shared from `@codaco/art/illustration/*`: the animation clock and easing (`motion`), and `BrowserWindow`, `AppTile`, `Badge`, `Cursor` and `ClickRing`, `DottedPath`, and `Confetti`. The clock can also play once and rest on its final frame (`useIllustrationClock(stillTime, { from: 0 })`), and `AppTile` can hold drawn content instead of an image.
