The Family Pedigree draws only the participant's family: the participant and
everyone connected to them through the stage's relationships, the same people
the Narrative Pedigree draws. People of the same type that other stages add,
such as friends or colleagues, are no longer drawn when the participant comes
back to the stage, and are never given labels, counted towards completeness or
offered to nomination prompts.

Nobody the participant draws can drop out of the family. Removing someone also
removes anyone connected to the participant only through them, and the
confirmation names those people first. A connection that is the only link
between the participant and someone cannot be disconnected; the participant is
told who would be left out and can connect them another way first.
