Refinements to the family pedigree:

- The side panel casts a clearer shadow over the family tree, and moving between its groups of questions no longer flashes a horizontal scrollbar. After the last required answer in a group, the answer shows a little longer before the next group slides in.
- The menu for adding a relative now opens on top of everything else on the screen, after the pointer rests on a person for a moment, and its buttons cast shadows.
- The family tree only zooms out by itself, to fit; zooming in is left to the participant (Show the whole family still fits the family fully). Selecting someone centres them together with their partners and children.
- The progress list shows what is done, ticked and struck through, as well as what is still needed.
- Long kinship words now break inside a symbol at the same places however the label was written: the break points are set in one place by the interview, and the stage wording carries none.
