## Limit how many alters can be named

<!-- section: nomination-limits -->
<!-- scene: name-generator-limits -->

Switch on this feature to set a minimum or maximum number of alters the participant can name. The limits count every alter named on the stage, across all of its prompts.

Once the participant reaches the maximum, they can't add any more alters and are prompted to continue to the next stage. Until they reach the minimum, they can't move past the last prompt.
