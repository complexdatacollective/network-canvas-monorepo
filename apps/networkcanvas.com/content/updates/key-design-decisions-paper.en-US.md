# Paper: key decisions in the design of Network Canvas

Self-reported network studies are often complex and burdensome, both during the interview and when managing the data afterwards. In this paper in Social Networks, the team describes the decisions behind the Network Canvas suite: Architect for designing interview protocols, Interviewer for administering them on touchscreens, and Server for managing deployment and exporting data.

Together, the tools give researchers with little technical expertise a complete workflow, from building their own network interviews to collecting the data in a secure central place. The paper explains the theoretical, philosophical and practical reasons for the key design choices, the experiences that guided them, and their strengths and limitations. [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socnet.2021.02.003).
