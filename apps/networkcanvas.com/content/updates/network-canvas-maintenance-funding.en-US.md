# Joshua Melville awarded two years of NIH funding to maintain Network Canvas

The National Institute on Drug Abuse has awarded Joshua Melville, Network Canvas's lead developer, an NIH Research Specialist award (R50) for research software engineers. It funds two years of work, from July 2026 to June 2028, to keep Network Canvas maintainable, secure and sustainable as open-science software.

The award builds on almost a decade of NIH funding for Network Canvas, which is now used in at least 55 NIH-funded studies, from opioid use disorder to social influences on microbial communities. Over the two years, Joshua will move the code to TypeScript and resolve key technical debt, enhance Architect, support offline interviewing with the web-based interview app, and grow the community around the software with new training resources. [Read the project details on NIH RePORTER](https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/11339209).
