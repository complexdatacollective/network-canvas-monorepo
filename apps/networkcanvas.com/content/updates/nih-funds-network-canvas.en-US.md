# NIH funds Network Canvas

The National Institute on Drug Abuse awards more than $3 million to build Network Canvas into a complete, free suite of tools for network data collection, in a collaboration between Northwestern University and the Oxford Internet Institute. [See the grant on NIH RePORTER](https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/9204151).

<!-- more -->

### Three tools, one workflow

The five-year grant set out to turn the touchscreen prototype from Joshua Melville's doctoral research and Northwestern's RADAR study into a complete suite: Architect, for designing network interviews without writing code; Interviewer, for conducting them on a touchscreen; and Server, for gathering and managing the data in one place.

### A collaboration across the Atlantic

Michelle Birkett and Gregory Phillips II led the project at Northwestern University with Patrick Janulis and Noshir Contractor, working with Bernie Hogan and Joshua Melville at the Oxford Internet Institute.

### Built with researchers

The team announced the award, and launched the first networkcanvas.com, in October 2016, with a plan to share early versions with researchers and shape the tools around their feedback. [Read Oxford's announcement](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/introducing-network-canvas-a-suite-of-tools-to-design-and-manage-social-network-analysis-studies/).
