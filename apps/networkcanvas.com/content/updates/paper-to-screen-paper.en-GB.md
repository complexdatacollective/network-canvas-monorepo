# Paper at CHI 2016: moving participant-aided sociograms from paper to screen

Key network measures for high-risk, hard-to-reach groups still have to be collected by asking people directly, and doing that on paper is slow and burdensome. This paper describes netCanvas, the first version of Network Canvas, which turns the paper participant-aided sociogram into a touchscreen tool. It simplifies data entry, needs less interviewer intervention and set-up, makes data easier to reuse, and shows the network as it is drawn.

The team tested it in a health behaviour study with a high-risk, hard-to-reach population, comparing paper and touchscreen with the same participants, and concluded that touchscreen network capture is now a viable alternative, even for highly sensitive data. [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/2858036.2858368).
