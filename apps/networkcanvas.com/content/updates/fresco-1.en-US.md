# Fresco 1.0

The first stable release of Fresco. Host it on your own server, upload protocols you designed in Architect, and let participants complete Network Canvas interviews in their web browser. [Read the release notes](https://github.com/complexdatacollective/Fresco/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

<!-- more -->

### Ready for research

Fresco opened as a public beta in March 2024. After testing by the community, version 1.0 removes the beta label: Fresco is ready for collecting research data.

### Interviews in the browser

Fresco gives participants the same look, feel and interfaces as Interviewer, and exports data in the same formats. Participants can join a study through a link, so interviews can take place remotely, and Fresco includes basic participant management and recruitment. It works with any protocol made in Architect.

### Try it first

The documentation includes a sandbox for exploring Fresco without deploying it, and step-by-step instructions for deploying your own copy. [Read the Fresco documentation](https://documentation.networkcanvas.com/en/collect-data/fresco/about).
