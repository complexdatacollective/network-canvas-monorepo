# Project Fresco funded: Interviewer in the web browser

An NIH supplement funds a 12-month pilot to bring Interviewer to the web browser, so participants can complete interviews remotely without installing an app. [Read the announcement](https://community.networkcanvas.com/t/17).

<!-- more -->

### Why the browser

COVID-19 moved public health Partner Services interviews onto the phone, and health departments needed a way to collect network data without meeting in person. Running Interviewer in the browser makes remote self-administration possible, and lets interviews take place where installing an app is not an option, such as on participants' own devices.

### What the pilot will change

Interviewer was designed for interviews alongside a trained researcher. To support participants working on their own, the project adds clearer guidance, stricter navigation and checks at each stage to protect data quality, and onboarding steps that can require a valid participant ID and show the agreements an ethics board requires.

### Your data, your server

Researchers stay in control of their data. The app runs on your own server or cloud account, and the Network Canvas team never collects or stores interview data. Like the rest of Network Canvas, it is developed in the open and free to use. [See the supplement on NIH RePORTER](https://reporter.nih.gov/project-details/10609285).
