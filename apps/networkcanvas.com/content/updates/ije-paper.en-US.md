# Paper: Network Canvas in the International Journal of Epidemiology

Social influence and contact networks are central to understanding health behaviour and how disease spreads, yet most survey software is not built to capture them. This paper introduces Network Canvas to epidemiologists as an end-to-end workflow for designing and conducting network interviews.

It describes the three applications, Architect, Interviewer and Server, which run on Windows, macOS and Linux, with Interviewer also on Android and iOS, and how their point-and-click design reduces the technical knowledge needed to collect network data. Network Canvas is open source and free under the GNU General Public License 3. [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyad036).
