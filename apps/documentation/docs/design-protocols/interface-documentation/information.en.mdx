The Information Interface is a general purpose screen that can be used to present information to participants using a variety of text and media resources. For example, this screen can be useful to introduce a study or to provide instructions or context for a particular stage in the survey.

When using the Information Interface, you can incorporate one or multiple [resources](/en/design-protocols/key-concepts/resources) by either retrieving files from your resource library in Architect or locally from your device. There is no limit on the number of items: participants can scroll through the screen, so add as many as you need.

## Configuring Information Screen

An information screen is made up of a required page heading, followed by a column of items shown from top to bottom. Each item can be text, an image, a video or audio, and a screen needs at least one. Items can be re-arranged by dragging. Images and videos can be given a display size: **Full size** shows them at their natural height, while **Small**, **Medium** and **Large** limit how tall they are drawn, keeping their shape.

### Best Practices

<GoodPractice>

Use this Interface to explain stage activities to participants to increase comprehension and engagement. This may be especially important for increasing survey accessibility.

</GoodPractice>

### Try to Avoid

<BadPractice>

Resist overloading the Interface with text and instead consider using other
resource types to communicate information to your participants.

</BadPractice>
